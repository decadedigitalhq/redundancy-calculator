Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Redundancy Calculator AU ("we", "us", "this site") handles information when you visit or use our website.

Information we collect

The redundancy calculator on this site runs entirely in your browser. The figures you enter (years of service, weekly pay) are not transmitted to our servers, stored, or logged — they exist only temporarily on your device to display your result.

We may automatically collect standard technical information when you visit, such as your browser type, device type, approximate location (derived from IP address), and pages visited, via analytics tools.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses or may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, to display ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

We may also use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) that use cookies to help us understand how visitors use this site. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

Third-party links

This site links to external resources such as the Fair Work Ombudsman. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect any information collected through this site, though no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
