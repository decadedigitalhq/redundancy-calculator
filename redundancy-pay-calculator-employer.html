Do you have to pay redundancy pay at all?

Small business exemption. Under the NES, an employer with fewer than 15 employees at the time doesn't have to pay redundancy pay. When you count:

  • Include employees of any associated entities (related companies).
  • Include casuals only if they're engaged on a regular and systematic basis.

The exemption covers redundancy pay only. Notice and unused annual leave are still owed. It also doesn't apply in some cases. For example, a business that becomes small by downsizing while bankrupt or in liquidation may still owe redundancy pay, and some awards have industry-specific redundancy schemes that cover small businesses.

Employees who don't get NES redundancy pay, whatever your size:

  • less than 12 months of continuous service
  • employed for a specific period, project or season
  • dismissed for serious misconduct
  • casual employees
  • apprentices, and trainees employed only for the length of the training arrangement

The employer's redundancy checklist

  1. Make sure it's a genuine redundancy. The job must no longer need to be done by anyone because of changes in the business. You must also have followed the consultation requirements in the award or enterprise agreement. Consider whether the employee could reasonably be redeployed elsewhere in the business, or in an associated entity. Read the genuine redundancy test →
  2. Making 15 or more employees redundant? Give Services Australia written notice of the proposed dismissals as soon as possible, and before the employees are made redundant.
  3. Give written notice. Employees entitled to NES notice must get it in writing. The notice period starts the day after you tell them. They can work it out, or you can pay it in lieu.
  4. Work out the final pay. Use the calculator above for redundancy pay, notice and annual leave. Annual leave is paid out at the same amount as if it were taken, including leave loading if they'd normally get it. Long service leave is separate and depends on state law. See how LSL works by state →
  5. Pay on time. Follow your award or agreement. The Fair Work Ombudsman says most awards require final pay within 7 days after the last day of employment.
  6. Withhold tax correctly. Genuine redundancy payments have a tax-free part, and the rest is taxed as an employment termination payment. See how redundancy pay is taxed →

Can't afford it, or found them another job? You can apply to the Fair Work Commission to reduce NES redundancy pay if you've found the employee other acceptable employment, or you can't pay the full amount.

Frequently asked questions

Do I pay notice and redundancy pay?

Yes, if the employee is entitled to both. They're separate NES entitlements. Notice covers the notice period, and redundancy pay compensates for the job loss. Small businesses are exempt from redundancy pay but still owe notice.

Is the calculation different for part-time employees?

The weeks are the same. You pay them at the part-timer's ordinary weekly pay. Enter their actual weekly ordinary pay in the calculator.

What if their award gives more than the NES?

Pay the higher amount. This calculator shows NES minimums only. Some awards, enterprise agreements and contracts give longer notice, more redundancy pay or extra entitlements.

The employee is in Victoria. Anything extra?

Long service leave. After 7 years, unused long service leave is paid out when employment ends. Our Victorian calculator includes it.
