Retraining after redundancy: free help first, then courses

Once you know what your payout is, the next question is usually "what now?" If a redundancy is your push to learn something new, start with the free help you're entitled to, then spend money on a course only where it fills a real gap.

Reviewed 26 November 2026 against Department of Employment and Workplace Relations and ATO guidance

Step 1: use the free help for retrenched workers

If you've been made redundant and have the right to work in Australia, the Australian Government's Workforce Australia employment services are free. Retrenched workers and their partners can register directly with a Workforce Australia provider from up to 3 months before to up to 6 months after the retrenchment date. You don't need to be on Centrelink payments or have a Centrelink referral. Providers can help with your résumé, job search, interview practice and access to training.

To find out what's available, call the Employment Services Information Line on 1800 805 260, or start at the government's What's Next site for retrenched workers.

Step 2: check whether a Free TAFE course fits

If you want a recognised qualification, check Free TAFE before paying for anything. Each state and territory sets its own eligibility rules, and people who are out of work are one of the priority groups. Course lists and eligibility differ by state, so check Your Career's Free TAFE page for your state.

Step 3: fill specific skill gaps with a short online course

For a particular skill a job ad keeps asking for, a short self-paced online course can be quicker than a formal qualification. The aim is to show you can do the thing on the job. It won't replace a licence or qualification your new field requires by law. Common gaps people fill after a redundancy:

  • Excel and spreadsheets. Almost every office role lists them. Excel courses on Udemy
  • Project management. Useful if you led work informally and want it on paper. Project management courses on Udemy
  • Bookkeeping and Xero. Handy for admin roles, or if you're going out on your own. Bookkeeping courses on Udemy
  • Data analysis. SQL, Power BI and similar tools are in demand across most industries. Data analysis courses on Udemy
  • Digital marketing. For marketing roles, or for promoting your own business. Digital marketing courses on Udemy

Before you buy, check the course's last-updated date, its reviews and its full content list. A course that's a few years old may teach an outdated version of the software.

Can I claim course costs on tax?

Usually not while you're between jobs. The ATO says you can't claim a self-education deduction if you're not employed when you pay the expense. You also can't claim study that's only to help you get a new job. The study has to be connected to the work you're doing when you pay for it. If you've started a new job and the course maintains or improves skills for that job, it may be different. Check the ATO's guidance or ask a registered tax agent before you rely on a deduction.

Don't forget your payout

If you're deciding how much to spend on retraining, it helps to know your final figure first. Use the redundancy payout calculator, and read how your payout is taxed so you know what actually lands in your account.
